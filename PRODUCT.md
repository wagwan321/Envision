# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and teams anywhere who need a website, custom software, an AI tool or a video, and are deciding which studio to trust with it. They arrive from a link, a referral or social media, usually on a phone, and judge the studio mostly by the work it shows. Envision presents itself as an international studio that works remotely (confirmed by the user); its current clients are in Lebanon.

## Product Purpose

The public website of Envision, an independent studio that builds websites, custom software, AI tools and video. It exists to show the studio's real client work convincingly and turn a visitor into a conversation. Success is a visitor who looks through the work and then books a 15-minute call or sends a project brief.

## Positioning

Envision builds the whole thing for a client, from design through development, 3D, multilingual content and booking or ordering flows, and shows it as live, working sites rather than mockups. The proof is the shipped client sites themselves.

## Operating Context

- Visitors browse the work, then either take the "plan your project" brief (a short quiz that ends in a details form and a Cal.com booking, `brief.js`) or use the contact form (opens an email).
- Cal.com booking link: `chris-el-haddad-067gwf/15min`, embedded inline after the brief.
- Static site (HTML, CSS, vanilla JS), repo `wagwan321/Envision`, `main` deploys to Vercel at envision-ruddy.vercel.app.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The opening 3D animation (orbiting EV logo, `hero-scene.js` and the intro in `index.html`) must stay. Everything else about the current look may be replaced.
- The brief quiz, the booking step and the contact form are working functions to keep, whatever they look like.
- Services offered: websites, custom software, AI tools, video and virtual tours.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Envision.
- The opening 3D animation with the orbiting EV mark (confirmed binding by the user).

## Evidence on Hand

Case studies, each a live site the studio built:

- **The Leisure Club** (Dik El-Mehdi, Lebanon; family country club since 1991): 15 pages, 3 languages, 43 weekly classes. Live: leisure-nu.vercel.app. Screens: `assets/work/leisure-*`.
- **Enfantillage** (Mazraat Yachouh, Lebanon; family toy store): 592 toys, 6 age groups, 5 ways to browse, e-commerce. Screens: `assets/work/enfantillage-*`.
- **Noma Living** (Hamra, Beirut, next to LAU; furnished student studios): 3D walk-through rendered in Blender (530 frames, day and night looks), 3 studio types, tour bookings. Screens: `assets/work/noma-*`.
- **First Nutrition** (official sports-supplement distributor, Amman and Lebanon): React storefront, 69 products from 21 brands, English and Arabic with right-to-left layout, Jordan/Lebanon country switch, WhatsApp cash-on-delivery ordering, a scroll-driven exploded view of a protein tub. Live: first-nutrition.vercel.app. Added to the showcase at the user's request.

Absent, and never to be invented: client testimonials or quotes, client logos beyond these four, awards, team photos, years in business, client counts or revenue figures.

## Product Principles

1. The work is the argument. Real, live client sites lead; claims follow from them.
2. Every case study states only facts that are true of the shipped site.
3. One clear next step: book a call or send a brief, reachable from anywhere on the page.
4. Built to the standard it sells: fast, accessible, and good on a phone.
